About Me

I am an aspiring IT Professional from India with a great interest in Linux systems, Networks and IT infrastructure.

My journey in this field started very early when I built my first computer using Raspberry Pi 3 Model-B in 8th grade - a project that ignited my passion for hands-on technical work.

I enjoy learning by building new projects, solving complex problems, and continuously improving myself. I'm ready to bring dedication, discipline, and practical knowledge to a professional IT environment.
